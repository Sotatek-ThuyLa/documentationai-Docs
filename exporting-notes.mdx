## 1. Step by step:

1. Open the note you wish to export.
2. Click the **Export icon** (upward arrow) in the top-right corner.
3. Select your content type: **Export Summary** or **Export Transcript**.
4. Choose your preferred file format: **Word (.docx)** or **PDF (.pdf)** to begin the download.

---

## **2. FAQ – Frequently Asked Questions**

### Q: What file formats does NoteX AI support for exporting data?

Currently, you can export your notes into the two most professional formats: Word (.docx) perfect for further editing, and PDF (.pdf) ideal for sending reports directly or permanent archiving.

### Q: Is there a page count or file size limit when exporting notes?

No. You can freely export notes of any length. Whether it is a short meeting or a hours-long seminar, NoteX AI smoothly processes the file export for your internal storage or for sharing with partners.

### Q: Will the exported file retain its original formatting (such as bullet points and headings)?

Yes. Professional text formatting like headings and bullet points will be fully preserved when converted to Word or PDF.
