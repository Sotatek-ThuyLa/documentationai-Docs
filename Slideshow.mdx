## **1. Step-by-Step Guide:**

1. Select the **Slideshow** tab on the left toolbar of your note.

2. Click **Create Slideshow**

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-baa369a2-6e04-46a8-b542-1fe4ce88714a/doc-9ea43233-156f-43f1-a7d0-c4a0b1ff5122/1780558321491-anax01d8bc-Slideshow1.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1440" height="900" alt="" />

3. In the **Slide Range** section, select the number of pages (Auto or within a range of 10-12 slides), and choose any **Template**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-baa369a2-6e04-46a8-b542-1fe4ce88714a/doc-9ea43233-156f-43f1-a7d0-c4a0b1ff5122/1780558226544-pdumlp3a8m-Slideshow2.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1440" height="900" alt="" />

4. After choosing your template and slide range, click **Generate slideshow**.

## **2. Use Cases**

- Quick weekly business performance report

- Summarize training content for employees.

## **3. FAQ: Frequently Asked Questions**

### **Q:** Can I export slides to edit on PowerPoint or Google Slides?

Absolutely. NoteX AI supports exporting files in **PPTX** format (preserving text formatting so you can easily continue editing on PowerPoint) or exporting as a **PDF** for quick sharing.

### **Q: What if the data in the note is too long?**

The AI will automatically filter and select the most crucial points to include in the Mindmap or Slideshow.

### **Q: Can I export this data?**

Yes, use the Export / Download feature.
