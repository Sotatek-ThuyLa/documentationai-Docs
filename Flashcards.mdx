## **1. Step-by-Step Creation Guide:**

1. Select the **Flashcards** tab on the left sidebar of your note.
2. Click **Create flashcards**
   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-baa369a2-6e04-46a8-b542-1fe4ce88714a/doc-9ea43233-156f-43f1-a7d0-c4a0b1ff5122/1780568423444-ixzgrgusd7n-Flashcard1.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1440" height="900" alt="" />
3. In the **Create flashcards** panel:
   - **Card count:** Select the number of flashcards.
   - **Card difficulty**: Select the difficulty level (Easy, Medium, Hard, or Mixed).
   - **Topic (optional):** You can enter specific points to emphasize or focus areas you want to review quickly (e.g., "Only focus on the discount section") to help the AI better understand the context.
     <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-baa369a2-6e04-46a8-b542-1fe4ce88714a/doc-9ea43233-156f-43f1-a7d0-c4a0b1ff5122/1780568456016-a6kds1ay2mm-Flashcard2.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1440" height="900" alt="" />
4. Click **Generate flashcards** to create a double-sided card set (Question - Answer).

## **4. FAQ: Frequently Asked Questions**

### **Q: What is the purpose of flashcards created from notes?**

Flashcards are the optimal method for memorizing terms, definitions, formulas, or new vocabulary. The AI will automatically extract "Concept - Definition" or "Term - Explanation" information pairs from your notes to create double-sided cards (Front contains the question/term, Back contains the answer/explanation) to help you quickly grasp the information.

### **Q: Can I export this data?**

Yes, use the Export / Download feature.
