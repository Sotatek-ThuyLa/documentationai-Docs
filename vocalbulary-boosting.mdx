## **1. How to Use:**

1. Click the **Vocabulary Boosting** icon in the top right corner within a note.
2. In **Terms & Names**, enter your keywords, proper names, or specialized terms (e.g., NoteX, Byeon, etc.).
3. After clicking **Add**, the new word will be saved immediately under the **Your Dictionary** section.
4. Hover over any added term and click the **x** icon to remove it.

## 2. Manual Management and Addition in Settings

1. On the main screen (Home), go to **Settings** in the bottom left corner.
2. Select **Vocabulary Boosting**.
3. In **Terms & Names**, enter your keywords, proper names, or specialized terms (e.g., NoteX, Byeon, etc.).
4. After clicking **Add**, the new word will be saved immediately under the **Your Dictionary** section.
5. Hover over any added term and click the **x** icon to remove it.

## **3. FAQ - Frequently Asked Questions**

### **Q: Can I share my vocabulary set with other members in my company?**

No. To ensure maximum personalization for each specific job role, this vocabulary set functions on a **User-specific** basis and is not shared between different accounts.

### **Q: Can I enter both English words and other languages into the same list?**

Absolutely. The system is designed to automatically identify the language of each keyword (Language detection per term), allowing it to group and feed them separately into the STT/LLM models accurately according to the context.

### **Q: If I switch my login device (from computer to phone), will my vocabulary set be lost?**

No. The vocabulary set is stored and automatically **synchronized in real-time** across all devices logged into your account.
