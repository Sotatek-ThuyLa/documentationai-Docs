## **1. Guide on How to Create and Use:**

1. Select the **Mindmap** tab on the left sidebar within your note.

2. Click **Create Mindmap** and choose from the available **Templates**. 

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-baa369a2-6e04-46a8-b542-1fe4ce88714a/doc-9ea43233-156f-43f1-a7d0-c4a0b1ff5122/1780547221858-xkl89gdu5w-mindmap-1.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1440" height="900" alt="" />

3. Click **Generate mindmap**; the AI will automatically analyze the text and draw a tree diagram.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-baa369a2-6e04-46a8-b542-1fe4ce88714a/doc-9ea43233-156f-43f1-a7d0-c4a0b1ff5122/1780547257472-k7qjm69lydh-mindmap-2.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1440" height="900" alt="" />

## **2. Use Cases**

- **Sales Manager:** Systemize customer feedback.

- **Project Manager:** By looking at the diagram, the Project Manager (PM) can instantly see the workflow.

- **Product Owner**: Manage features & create product roadmaps.

- **Operation Manager:** Outline new operating procedures.

- **Marketing Manager**: Campaign planning.

---

## **3. FAQ: Frequently Asked Questions**

### **Q: What if the data in the note is too long?** 

The AI will automatically filter and select the most crucial points to include in the Mindmap or Slideshow.

### **Q: Can I export this data?** 

Yes, use the Export / Download feature.

### **Q: What types of content can I use to create a mindmap?**

Any type of content on NoteX AI! From raw text and long articles to meeting transcriptions (Live transcription)—all can be transformed into a visual mindmap in just a few seconds.
