## **1. Step-by-Step Guide**

**Step 1:** Open the note you want to share.

**Step 2:** Click the **Share** icon in the top right corner.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-baa369a2-6e04-46a8-b542-1fe4ce88714a/doc-9ea43233-156f-43f1-a7d0-c4a0b1ff5122/1780542879974-8hu7284n64n-youtube.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1440" height="900" alt="" />

**Step 3:** Turn on **Public** mode.

**Step 4:** Customize privacy settings:

- **Select Tab:** Select the sections you want the recipient to see (e.g., only show _Summary_ and _Mindmap_, and hide _Transcript_).
- **Add Password:** Enable password protection to secure sensitive content.

**Step 5:** Copy the link and send it to your partner.
