## **1. Step-by-Step Creation Guide:**

1. Select the **Quizzes** tab on the left sidebar of your note.
2. Click **Create quizzes**
   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-baa369a2-6e04-46a8-b542-1fe4ce88714a/doc-9ea43233-156f-43f1-a7d0-c4a0b1ff5122/1780564089071-jwhxfk9aoxd-quiz-1.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1440" height="900" alt="" />
3. In the **Create quizzes** panel:
   - **Quiz count:** Select the number of questions.
   - **Quiz difficulty**: Select the difficulty level (Easy, Medium, Hard, or Mixed).
   - **Topic (optional):** You can enter specific points to emphasize or focus areas you want to test (e.g., "Only focus on the discount section") to help the AI better understand the context.
     <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-baa369a2-6e04-46a8-b542-1fe4ce88714a/doc-9ea43233-156f-43f1-a7d0-c4a0b1ff5122/1780564125971-1oyrari5h1e-quiz-2.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1440" height="900" alt="" />
4. Click **Generate quizzes** to create.

## 2. How to Take a Quiz:

1. Answer the questions one by one until finished.
   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-baa369a2-6e04-46a8-b542-1fe4ce88714a/doc-9ea43233-156f-43f1-a7d0-c4a0b1ff5122/1780564215293-dd637trg3ac-quiz-4.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1440" height="900" alt="" />
   <Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
     **Tip:** Check the **Transcript Context** section below each question to see the quoted text segment from your original notes.
   </Callout>
2. After completing the quiz, you can choose to try again (**Retry Quiz**) or generate a new set of questions (**Generate New**).

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-baa369a2-6e04-46a8-b542-1fe4ce88714a/doc-9ea43233-156f-43f1-a7d0-c4a0b1ff5122/1780564189223-vvyybh6vjbl-quiz-3.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1440" height="900" alt="" />

- **Retry Quiz:** Restart the quiz from the beginning (unlimited retries).
- **Generate New:** Generate a brand-new set of quiz questions.

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
  **Note:** Each user gets 1 quiz creation turn; Pro users get 3 turns.
</Callout>

3. Review and manage (rename, retry, save) your created quizzes in the **Version** section.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-baa369a2-6e04-46a8-b542-1fe4ce88714a/doc-9ea43233-156f-43f1-a7d0-c4a0b1ff5122/1780564476052-qlw9yxlli1j-quiz-5.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1440" height="900" alt="" />

## **4. FAQ: Frequently Asked Questions**

### Q: Does this feature grade the quiz and save my progress history?

Yes. Upon completing the quiz, you will receive an instant score, with correct/incorrect answers shown after each attempt, accompanied by detailed explanations for each question. Your quiz set will be saved in the Version history.

### Q: What can I customize when creating a quiz?

You can fully customize your quiz by:

- Choosing the number of questions to generate (5, 10, 20 questions...).
- Selecting the difficulty level: Easy, Medium, Hard, or Mixed to challenge yourself.
- Adding an optional Topic to focus on a specific segment of content within your notes.

### **Q: Can I export this data?**

Yes, you can use the Export feature to download the quiz in PDF (.pdf) and Word (.docx) formats.
