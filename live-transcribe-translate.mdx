## **1. Use Cases:**

- **Meetings with Foreign Partners:** Understand the content instantly without the need for an interpreter.

- **Lecture/Seminar Note-taking:** Save all important content without worrying about missing information.

- **Interviews & Research:** Automate audio transcription, saving hours of manual work.

---

## **2. Step-by-Step Guide**

### **Step 1: Initialization**

On the main interface (Home) of NoteX, click the **Live translation** button (at the bottom of the screen) to open the recording control panel.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-baa369a2-6e04-46a8-b542-1fe4ce88714a/doc-9ea43233-156f-43f1-a7d0-c4a0b1ff5122/1780475953787-ho4fxd2y6qt-translation.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1832" height="1082" alt="" />

### **Step 2: Select Translation Mode**

In the **Live translation** window, you need to check the following options:

- **Live transcribe:** Toggle the switch to the right to enable the real-time running text feature.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-baa369a2-6e04-46a8-b542-1fe4ce88714a/doc-9ea43233-156f-43f1-a7d0-c4a0b1ff5122/1780476251886-0cznevgk1f2m-live-transcribe2.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1768" height="1102" alt="" />

- **Folder:** Select the folder to store the recording.

- **Speech Languages:** Select the source language that you are speaking/hearing.

- **Translation language:** Select the language you want to translate into (e.g., English or Vietnamese).

* **Audio Setting:** Ensure your Microphone is connected and receiving signal.

* **Additional instructions (opt.):&#x20;**Add specific focus or requests to help the AI understand the context better (can be added or skipped).

### **Step 3: Start and Monitor**

Click **Start Record**. The screen will be split into 2 parts:

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-baa369a2-6e04-46a8-b542-1fe4ce88714a/doc-9ea43233-156f-43f1-a7d0-c4a0b1ff5122/1780477337101-h722ze1g6gp-empty.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1440" height="900" alt="" />

- **Left side:** Displays the original text transcribed from the speech.

- **Right side:** Displays the live translation into your selected language.

### **Step 4: Completion**

During the recording process, you can click **Pause** or **Reset** to start over. Once finished, click **Create Note** to save the entire content to your NoteX account.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-baa369a2-6e04-46a8-b542-1fe4ce88714a/doc-9ea43233-156f-43f1-a7d0-c4a0b1ff5122/1780477507849-tjckwy41bvc-ouput.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1440" height="900" alt="" />

## **3. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)**

### Q: Can I choose multiple translation languages at the same time?

- Currently, the system supports selecting up to 05 translation languages per recording session to ensure the highest accuracy.

### Q: What if the microphone disconnects midway?

- You can check the Audio Setting section at the bottom left corner of the recording screen to reselect the input device without closing the session.

### Q: What factors does the translation quality depend on?

- Accuracy depends on network speed, source volume, and the clarity of the speaker's words. You should use a good quality microphone for optimal results.
