## **1. How to use:**

1. Select the **Podcast** / **Short** tab from the menu on the left sidebar of your note.

2. Click **Create** **Podcast** / **Short**.

3. **Duration:** Choose the duration (30s, 60s, 90s, 180s).

4. **Voice:** Select a suitable voice (Olivia, Puck,...) and press the **Play** button to preview.

5. **For Podcast - Dual Speakers:** Toggle the switch on the right if you want to create a dialogue between two people (making the content more engaging).

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-baa369a2-6e04-46a8-b542-1fe4ce88714a/doc-9ea43233-156f-43f1-a7d0-c4a0b1ff5122/1780565377057-i63r12hiw7o-podcast.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1440" height="900" alt="" />

6. **For Short:**

   - **Template:** Choose the video background.

   - **Video captions:** Toggle the switch on the right if you want text captions in the video.

     <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-baa369a2-6e04-46a8-b542-1fe4ce88714a/doc-9ea43233-156f-43f1-a7d0-c4a0b1ff5122/1780565319911-u7d1yw9okh-setting.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1440" height="900" alt="" />

7. Click **Generate Podcast** / **Short** to create.

## 2. Create and manage Podcasts / Shorts:

1. Click the **<kbd>+</kbd>** button in the top right corner of the **Podcast / Short** interface to create a new version.

2. Select the **Version** button to review and edit (rename, save) previously created versions.

## **4. FAQ: Frequently Asked Questions**

### Q: Will the AI-converted Podcast sound "robotic" or stiff?

No, NoteX AI uses the most advanced Text-to-Speech (TTS) technology, creating conversations with natural intonation, proper pauses, and pitch variations like a real person. You can even choose the "2-speaker dialogue" (Co-host) format for a more dynamic listening experience.

### Q: Can I download the Podcast audio or the Short video file?

Yes, the system allows you to download high-quality audio files in **MP3** format (**Podcast**) and videos in **MP4** format (**Short**) for archiving, offline listening, or sharing on any platform.

### **Q: What is special about the Shorts (Short Video) feature?**

It allows you to create videos based on note content but with backgrounds from popular games (like GTA, Subway Surfers) to increase entertainment value when sharing on social media.
