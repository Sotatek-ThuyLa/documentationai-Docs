## **Step 1: Choose your login method**

On the main <a href="https://notexapp.com/signin" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">**Log in to NoteX AI**</a> screen, there are 4 options available:

- **Use a Google, Apple, or Microsoft account:** Fast login via your existing corporate or personal account.

- **Continue with Email:** Log in using a separate email account created on NoteX.

## **Step 2: Enter your credentials (If Email is selected)**

If you choose the Email method, the system will redirect you to the **Log in to your account** screen:

1. **Email:** Enter your work email address (For example: <a href="mailto:thuy.la@sotatek.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">[notex@gmail.com](mailto:notex@gmail.com)</a>).

2. **Password:** Enter your established password.

3. **Login:** Click the black button to access your dashboard.

## **Step 3: Passwordless Login (Optional)**

If you prefer not to manage multiple passwords, you can select **Login without Password**. The system will send a verification code or a direct access link to your email.

---

## **FAQ: Frequently Asked Questions**

### 1. What should I do if I forget my password?

On the password entry screen, click on the Forgot Password? text. The system will guide you through resetting a new password via email within 30 seconds.

### 2. Can I switch between Google and Microsoft accounts?

Yes. However, please note that each login method may create an independent account unless you use the exact same root email address.

### 3. Why haven't I received the Passwordless Login email?

Please check your Spam or Promotion folders. If you still don't see it, wait 1 minute and click resend.
