## **1. What is AI Meeting Bot used for?**

**Use-cases:**

- **Executive Management:** Keep track of critical decisions via summaries without having to attend every single meeting.
- **Sales Teams:** Focus entirely on listening to clients instead of talking and taking manual notes simultaneously.
- **Operations:** Accurately archive action items (Tasks) and deadlines discussed during the meeting.

---

## **2. How to use the AI Meeting Bot feature**

You can use the Bot in two ways: Manually or Automatically.

### **Method 1: Live Meeting - Manual Bot Creation (Instant Use)**

1. On the **Home** screen, select **Live Meeting**.
   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-baa369a2-6e04-46a8-b542-1fe4ce88714a/doc-9ea43233-156f-43f1-a7d0-c4a0b1ff5122/1780472784202-bydld4xbd8d-Tutorial-MB.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1512" height="898" alt="" />
2. Paste the meeting link (Google Meet, Zoom...) into the box **"Paste the meeting invite link here"**.
   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-baa369a2-6e04-46a8-b542-1fe4ce88714a/doc-9ea43233-156f-43f1-a7d0-c4a0b1ff5122/1780473195561-8r0r2xnc1xj-tuto-MB-2.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1548" height="970" alt="" />
3. Select the meeting language in the **Transcript Language** section (e.g., Vietnamese).
4. Click **AI Summary** the Bot will automatically request to join the meeting room to start note-taking.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-baa369a2-6e04-46a8-b542-1fe4ce88714a/doc-9ea43233-156f-43f1-a7d0-c4a0b1ff5122/1780473376920-31pfcc88w9w-Tuto-MB-3.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1656" height="1032" alt="" />

### **Method 2: Auto-join Bot - Automatic Bot Setup (For busy schedules)**

To have the Bot automatically join scheduled meetings on your calendar:

1. **Connect Calendar:** Go to the left menu **Upcoming Meetings** - click **Connect with Calendar** to sync with your Google Calendar.
   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-baa369a2-6e04-46a8-b542-1fe4ce88714a/doc-9ea43233-156f-43f1-a7d0-c4a0b1ff5122/1780473895777-81v0ge5s1y3-calender.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1568" height="978" alt="" />
2. **Customize per meeting:** In the **Upcoming Meetings** list, you can toggle the **Auto-join** switch for specific meetings you want the Bot to join.
   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-baa369a2-6e04-46a8-b542-1fe4ce88714a/doc-9ea43233-156f-43f1-a7d0-c4a0b1ff5122/1780474152454-qcc2sr4gitg-auto-join.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1598" height="996" alt="" />
3. **Enable automatic join for all:** Go to **Settings** > **Meeting Bot** > Turn on **"Automatically record online meetings from calendar events"**.
   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-baa369a2-6e04-46a8-b542-1fe4ce88714a/doc-9ea43233-156f-43f1-a7d0-c4a0b1ff5122/1780474448014-j9r7jqo7k1l-Calender.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1612" height="1002" alt="" />

## **3. FAQ - Frequently Asked Questions**

### **Q: What name will the Bot display in the meeting?**

A: By default, the Bot will be named "NoteX Bot" or "\[Your Name]'s NoteX AI Bot". You can change this name in **Settings > Meeting Bot**.

### **Q: Do I need to keep the NoteX tab open during the entire meeting?**

A: No. Once the Bot has entered the room, it will run independently. You can close your browser, and the results will appear in the **All Notes** section right after the meeting ends.
