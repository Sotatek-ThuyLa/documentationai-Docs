## **1. What is Live Recording used for?**

**Use cases:**

- **Record live meetings:** Capture the entire meeting and receive an AI summary as soon as it ends.
- **Record lectures:** Turn lectures into well-structured notes for easier review.
- **Capture ideas:** Save ideas or voice notes anytime, anywhere.
- **Interviews & research:** Record interviews and quickly extract key information.

---

## **2. How to Use Live Recording**

### **Step 1: Start Recording**

1. From the **Home** screen, select **Live Recording**.
2. Choose the **recording language** that matches your content.
3. Click **Start Recording**.
4. Your browser will request microphone access. Select **Allow** to begin recording.

> **Note:** Make sure your microphone is working properly before starting.

---

### **Step 2: Stop Recording**

1. When finished, click **Stop Recording**.
2. NoteX will automatically upload the recording and begin AI processing.
3. Processing may take anywhere from a few seconds to several minutes depending on the recording length.

---

### **Step 3: View the Results**

Once processing is complete, your recording will be converted into an AI note containing:

- AI Summary and structured AI notes
- Transcript
- Key points
- Action items (if available)

You can also generate:

- Mindmap
- Slideshow
- Flashcards
- Quiz
- Podcast
- Shorts
- Translate into another language
- And many more AI-powered formats

---

## **3. Manage Your Recording**

After the note is created, you can:

- Edit the note content.
- Rename the note.
- Share it with others.
- Move it to a Folder.
- Translate it into another language (supports over 100 languages).
- Export it as PDF, Word, or Markdown.
- Continue using Nova Agent to generate emails, documents, or explore your note in greater depth.

## **4. FAQ - Frequently Asked Questions**

### **Q: How long can I record?**

A: The maximum recording duration is 5 hours. In addition, your available recording time depends on your subscription plan and the remaining Credits in your account.

### **Q: Can I close my browser while recording?**

A: No. Your browser must remain open throughout the recording. If you close the tab or browser, the recording session will end.

### **Q: Can I use Live Recording on my phone?**

A: Yes. Live Recording works on both desktop and mobile browsers as long as the browser supports microphone access.

### **Q: Where is my recording stored after processing?**

A: Once AI processing is complete, your note will appear in **All Notes**, where you can edit, share, or transform it into many other formats.
