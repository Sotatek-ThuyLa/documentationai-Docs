## Quick Start

Set up and get started in just a few minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an Account" icon="user-plus" title-type="p">
    Create a free account at [https://notexapp.com/home](https://notexapp.com/home) — No credit card required.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Join a Meeting" icon="video" title-type="p">
    Start or join any supported meeting (online or offline). NoteX AI will begin recording and generate AI summaries automatically.
  </Step>

  <Step title="View Results" icon="file-text" title-type="p">
    Edit, share, and transform your notes into mind maps, slides, podcasts, and many other AI-powered formats. Explore your content further with Nova Agent to create emails, documents, and more. [https://notexapp.com/app](https://notexapp.com/app).
  </Step>
</Steps>
