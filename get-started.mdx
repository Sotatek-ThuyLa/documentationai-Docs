## Quick Start

Set up and use in just a few minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up for an account" icon="user-plus" title-type="p">
    Create a free account at <a href="https://notexapp.com/home" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">[https://notexapp.com/home](https://notexapp.com/home)</a> - No credit card required.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Join a meeting" icon="video" title-type="p">
    Start or join any supported meeting. NoteX AI automatically activates and begins recording.
  </Step>

  <Step title="View results" icon="file-text" title-type="p">
    Access your recordings, translations, and summaries at [<a href="https://notexapp.com/app" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">https://notexapp.com/app</a>](https://notexapp.com/app).
  </Step>
</Steps>
